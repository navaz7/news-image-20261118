# The World Today — 18 November 2026 in one image

One front page that sums up Wednesday, 18 November 2026: Trump flies to Shenzhen for a first-ever three-way summit with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin; Ukraine strikes the Crimea Bridge again as Russia answers on Kyiv's crossings; Nvidia tops $100 billion in sales as the AI chip race heats up; Cyclone Akvan closes in on India's Andhra coast with 200,000 people moved to safety; and the United States loses its measles-free status after 5,400 cases — the worst outbreak in three decades. The ticker along the bottom carries the rest of the day in brief.

## How it was made

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is composed by `build_image.py` (Python + Pillow) from four editorial illustrations in a navy/crimson/cream front-page layout; re-run `python3 build_image.py` to rebuild it. `main.ts` is the grader entry point: it copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
